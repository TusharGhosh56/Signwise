# UI, Copy & Design Rules: No Marketing Clutter & No Fake Badges

1. **PROHIBITED: Fake Trust Badges & Reassurance Spam**
   - NEVER invent or display fake authenticity badges (e.g., `"100% Authentic"`, `"Verified Review"`, `"100% Guaranteed"`).
   - NEVER add random marketing filler bullets under CTAs or banners (e.g., `"Instant turnaround"`, `"Zero legal retainer required"`, `"Instant payout"`, `"Free first review"`).
   - NEVER manufacture fake vanity metrics (e.g., `"14,000+ contracts scanned"`, `"92% success rate"`) without explicit real data.

2. **PROHIBITED: "Chip Fatigue" & Over-Badging**
   - Do NOT stack multiple colorful pill chips, badges, and status boxes on a single card.
   - Cards should be clean, typographic, and elegant. Avoid surrounding every label or status in a pill container with borders and background fills.
   - Let content breathe. Use clean typography hierarchy, subtle divider lines, and whitespace instead of endless chips.

3. **Aesthetic Tone: Executive, Serious, Quiet Confidence**
   - Signwise is a high-end legal intelligence platform. Speak with the understated authority of an institutional publication or enterprise legal tool (like Harvey, Ironclad, or Stripe).
   - Avoid aggressive SaaS marketing tropes, hustle-culture buzzwords, or cheesy sales pitches.
