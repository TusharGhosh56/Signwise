# Signwise

> **"Know what you're agreeing to before you sign."**  
> Plain-English contract analysis, risk detection, obligation mapping, and negotiation intelligence.

## Overview
Signwise helps individuals understand agreements (employment, rental, freelance, loan, NDA, etc.) without overwhelming legal jargon. It breaks down documents into:
- 🚦 **Traffic-Light Assessment**: Straightforward (🟢), Review Recommended (🟡), or High Attention (🔴).
- 🧩 **4-Box Clause Breakdown**: *What it says*, *What it means*, *Why it matters*, and *What you could ask*.
- ✋ **"What Am I Giving Up?"**: Clear summary of surrendered rights (IP assignments, restrictive covenants, notice periods).
- 📋 **Bilateral Obligations**: What *You* must do vs what *They* must do.
- 💵 **Financial Terms**: Base, variable pay, deposits, penalties, and lock-ins.
- 📑 **Synchronized Viewer**: Side-by-side original contract viewer and clause explanations with bidirectional highlight and jump-to-section.
- 💬 **Grounded Q&A & Negotiation**: Ask questions with section references and 1-click HR clarification email generator.
